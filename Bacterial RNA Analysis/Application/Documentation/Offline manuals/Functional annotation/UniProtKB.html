UniProtKB functional annotation

Bacterial RNA Analysis can query UniProtKB by gene or locus-tag identifiers and retrieve protein descriptions, gene names, organism information, and Gene Ontology annotations. Reviewed UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot is the recommended default; the broader UniProtKB option also includes TrEMBL.

Identifier lookup

SettingPurpose
Organism name or taxonomy IDRestricts gene and locus-tag searches to the intended organism and avoids ambiguous bacterial gene names.
Reviewed onlyPrioritizes manually reviewed Swiss-Prot annotations.
Reviewed + TrEMBLExpands coverage with computationally annotated UniProtKB entries.

Retrieved information

Matched accession, protein name, gene names, organism, and GO biological-process, molecular-function, and cellular-component terms are written to the Functional annotation folder and included in the analysis workbook.
